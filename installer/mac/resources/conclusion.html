OJU is installed

Open your DAW and look for OJU under Made by Joseph:

  • Logic Pro: Audio FX > Audio Units > Made by Joseph > OJU
  • Ableton Live: Plug-ins > VST3 > Made by Joseph (or Audio Units)
  • Reaper: FX browser, search "OJU"

If it doesn't show up, rescan plugins (Ableton: Settings > Plug-ins > Rescan; Reaper: Settings > Plug-ins > Re-scan) or restart the DAW.

Then put OJU on your vocal, press the brass eye, and sing.
